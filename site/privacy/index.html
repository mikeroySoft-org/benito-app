Privacy policy

Benito collects no data: “Data Not Collected” on the App Store.

  1. Data Not Collected

    We collect nothing from the app. No analytics, ads, crash reports or tracking code. No company server, and no account with us.

  2. Where your data lives

    On your Apple devices, and on your family’s own server if you add one. Export JSON or CSV any time.

  3. Who can see it

    Only people you invite. Each phone has its own Secure Enclave key. Remove someone and their phone stops syncing.

  4. Deleting your data

    In the app, Account › Delete account removes your account from your family’s server. Account › Erase all data clears an iPhone used on its own. We hold nothing to delete.

  5. Apple services

    Live Activity updates can use Apple’s push service, with your server’s own key. Siri follows Apple’s policy.

  6. Invite links

    The one-time secret sits after the “#”, which browsers never send to a server. Your server keeps only its hash.

  7. This website

    No cookies, analytics or trackers, and fonts are served from here. GitHub Pages may keep standard server logs.

  8. Changes

    Last updated 24 November 2026. Changes appear here with a new date. Questions: Support.

Benito is free and MIT licensed. No ads, no analytics, no accounts with us.

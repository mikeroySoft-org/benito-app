Privacy

Benito collects no data. On the App Store this is "Data Not Collected".

The app

What you log stays on your iPhone, or on a family server that you run. We don't operate a server, and the app has no analytics, ads, tracking or third-party SDKs.

This website

No cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub Pages hosts it and may keep standard server logs.

Invite links

The invite in a link sits after the "#", so your browser never sends it to this website. The join page reads it on your phone only.

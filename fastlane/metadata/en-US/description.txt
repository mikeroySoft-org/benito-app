Benito is a baby tracker for the newborn months: feeds, sleep and diapers, logged in one or two taps and shared with everyone who looks after your baby.

It has no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no analytics. There is no company cloud. Your baby's log stays on your iPhone, or on a small server your family runs itself.

LOG IN A TAP
• Nursing timer with left and right sides, pause and resume
• Bottles with ounces, breast milk or formula
• Diapers: wet, dirty or both
• Sleep and naps, by timer or entered afterwards
• Pumping, medicine, walks, car rides and crying
• Fix mistakes: edit or delete any entry, or log one at an earlier time

SEE THE DAY AT A GLANCE
• Home shows time since the last feed and diaper, and whether your baby is asleep
• Day and week timelines
• Daily totals for feeds, ounces, diapers and sleep, ready for the pediatrician
• Running timers on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and in the Apple Watch Smart Stack
• Siri and Shortcuts: start a feed, start a nap, log a diaper

SHARE WITH YOUR FAMILY
• Every phone sees the same timeline, and timers started on one phone run on the others
• Invite a partner, grandparent or babysitter by Messages, AirDrop, QR code or SharePlay
• No usernames or passwords for family: each iPhone gets its own key, kept in the Secure Enclave
• Caregiver invites can end after a few hours or days
• Sharing uses the Benito server, a small open-source program you run on a Mac, a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or a VPS. Setup guide: https://benito.mikeroysoft.com/self-host/

OR USE IT ON ONE IPHONE
Choose "This iPhone only" (or "This iPad only") and Benito works with no server and no account. Connect a server later and your history comes along.

YOUR DATA
• Data Not Collected: the developer receives nothing from the app
• Export everything as JSON or CSV at any time
• Open source under the MIT license: https://github.com/mikeroysoft-org/benito-app

Benito is a logbook. It does not give medical advice. Ask your pediatrician about your baby's health.

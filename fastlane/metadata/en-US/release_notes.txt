This update keeps your family’s phones in sync when you’re away from home, and it tidies a few everyday details:

• If the saved server address can’t be reached, Benito automatically tries the same server on your home network.
• The Apple Watch Smart Stack timer looks right, with controls that stay readable on the plate.
• The Home activity bar no longer repeats the last-feed line already shown above it.
• When someone else stops a running timer, the Live Activity on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and Watch Smart Stack ends even if the app isn’t open.
